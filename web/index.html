Microbial functional dark matter

Putting a calibrated number on the unknown.

Most microbial genes have no known function and no ground truth to check against. Obscuron scores how far each one sits beyond characterised sequence space, with a checked error rate, then proves the score works by rewinding the reference database in time.

The problem

A fifth of the microbial world has no name.

Function is normally assigned by similarity to something already characterised. A gene with no similar known sequence gets nothing, so it stays dark.

Across a 502-genome, phylum-stratified survey, more than one in five proteins carried no Pfam family at the snapshot boundary. The deeper obstacle is evaluation: with no labels, you cannot even tell whether a novelty detector is right.

Obscuron manufactures a real test set by freezing the reference at an earlier snapshot and grading what was dark then against what has since been characterised.

The approach

Three ideas doing the work.

Not annotation. A calibrated statement of how far a gene sits beyond the known, validated on a benchmark built to resist the one thing that quietly inflates every temporal holdout.

Scoring

Calibrated novelty

Distance from characterised space in a protein-language-model embedding, passed through an extreme-value tail fit so the output is a checked false-flag rate, not a hand-set cutoff.

Validation

Retrospective proof

Freeze the reference at T₀, score what was dark then, and grade those scores against T₁, where some genes have since been characterised. Real labels, no manual annotation.

Rigour

Leakage control

The headline runs on ESM-2, pretrained before the snapshot, so it cannot have seen the answer. A genomic model that saw the window is kept as the controlled comparison arm.

The method · five evidence layers

One frontier, seen five independent ways.

The framework rests on convergence: a candidate is trustworthy when independent lines of evidence agree. Layer 1 carries the headline; the rest corroborate it from angles that share no inputs.

What this does and does not claim

The guardrails are part of the method, not a caveat bolted on afterward.

Prioritisation, not determination
Every score ranks how far a gene sits beyond known space. It never asserts a function.
The headline is leakage-clean
ESM-2 was pretrained before the snapshot; the genomic model is quarantined to the controlled comparison.
Comparisons are matched-scale
The three arms are scored on identical family sets; some arms run on subsampled families where compute is bound.
Retrospective, not novel in itself
Temporal holdout is established practice. The contribution is the leakage control layered on top of it.
